Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Here are some common inquiries from our prospective clients.

How long does a typical software project take?
Project timelines vary greatly depending on complexity. A simple web application might take 4-6 weeks, while a complex enterprise cloud migration or custom CRM can take 3-6 months. We provide detailed timeline estimates during our discovery phase.
Do you provide ongoing support after launch?
Absolutely. We believe that deployment is just the beginning. We offer various tiered SLA (Service Level Agreement) packages that include 24/7 monitoring, security patching, bug fixes, and feature updates.
Who owns the intellectual property (IP) to the code?
You do. Once the project is fully paid and delivered, 100% of the intellectual property, source code, and assets are transferred to your company. We act purely as a work-for-hire agency.
Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?
Yes. We take confidentiality very seriously. We are happy to sign your company's standard NDA before we even have our first consultation call.

Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

Last Updated: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through our website, as well as any related services, sales, marketing, or events. Please read this privacy policy carefully as it will help you understand what we do with the information that we collect.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the website, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and services, or otherwise contact Us. The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the website.

  • Personal Information Provided by You: We collect names; phone numbers; email addresses; mailing addresses; job titles; contact preferences; and other similar information.
  • Automatically Collected Information: We automatically collect certain information when you visit, use or navigate the website. This information does not reveal your specific identity (like your name or contact information) but may include device and usage information, such as your IP address, browser, and device characteristics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for purposes based on legitimate business interests, the fulfillment of our contract with you, compliance with our legal obligations, and/or your consent. Specifically, we use the information we collect or receive to:

  • Respond to user inquiries and offer support.
  • Send administrative information to you, such as product, service, and new feature information.
  • Fulfill and manage your requests, orders, and contracts.
  • Deliver targeted advertising to you based on your interests and location.

3. Will Your Information Be Shared?

We only share information with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill business obligations. We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade any of your information with third parties for promotional purposes.

4. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this privacy policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as tax, accounting, or other legal requirements).

5. How We Keep Your Information Safe

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process, including AES-256 encryption and strict access controls. However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
